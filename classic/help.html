Help & FAQ

Straight answers on discounts, shipping, returns and fit. If it's not here, it's not a thing yet.

Student discount

Use code STUDENT15 in your bag to take 15% off everything, including sale items. Sign up with your .edu email and we’ll send it to you so you don’t have to remember it.

Shipping

Orders over $50 ship free. Under $50, shipping is a flat $6.95. Your bag shows how close you are to free shipping.

Returns & exchanges

Wrong size or not your color? Send it back within 30 days for a free return or exchange.

Sizing & fit

The Trail Cargo Pants come in XS to XXL with a baggy, relaxed fit and a 90% nylon, 10% spandex blend. Check each product page for its sizes and materials.

About this site

This is a practice storefront built for a class project. Browse, fill your bag and try the checkout. No real orders are placed and no payment is taken.
