About ChiActive

We make clothing and gear for college students who want to keep up with city life and outdoor adventures, without owning two wardrobes.

Our story

One city. Four seasons. Zero time to change.

Student life in Chicago doesn’t slow down for the weather. You leave for an 8 a.m. class in a cold drizzle, eat lunch in the sun, and catch the train home in a wind that seems to come from every direction at once.

ChiActive started with a simple idea: your clothes should be ready for all of it. Practical, youthful gear that moves from the lecture hall to the lakefront to a weekend on the trail, and looks good doing it.

The six-pointed red star on our patch comes straight from the Chicago flag. The mountains under it are for every adventure that starts the moment you leave the city limits.

Built for the four-season city.

Who we make gear for

Students who balance it all.

You’re juggling classes, a commute, maybe a job, and still finding time to get outside. We design for that day.

  • Commuters who ride the CTA, bike to class or walk across campus in every kind of weather.
  • Weekend explorers heading to the dunes, forest preserves or a quick trip out of the city.
  • Budget-minded students who want gear that does more than one job.
