Built for campus. Ready for Chicago.

Gear reviewSeptember 23, 2026by Vaidehi Chavda4 min read

The ChiActive Urban Campus Backpack is designed for college students who need a reliable backpack for classes, commuting, biking, studying outdoors and exploring Chicago.

Chicago weather can change quickly. From light rain and wind to busy train rides and long walks across campus, students need a backpack that can keep up with their everyday routine. The Urban Campus Backpack combines durability, comfort, storage and weather protection in one modern design.

Weather-resistant exterior

25-liter capacity

Made for Chicago college life

Whether you’re walking through campus, riding the CTA, biking to class or spending the afternoon studying near the lakefront, the Urban Campus Backpack is designed to move with you.

Its simple black design works with almost any style while giving you the practical features you need every day.

The lake wind doesn’t care about your plans. Your backpack should.

Chicago weather can change quickly

Chicago students experience everything from sunny afternoons to rain, wind, snow and changing temperatures throughout the school year. When you’re carrying a laptop, textbooks, notebooks, headphones and chargers, unexpected weather becomes a problem. A weather-resistant backpack adds a layer of protection against light rain and moisture.

Designed for commuting

Chicago has thousands of students who commute using trains, buses, bikes or a combination of all three. With about 25 liters of storage and a lightweight 1.8-pound design, this backpack has plenty of room for daily essentials without being bulky.

Useful beyond the classroom

Chicago offers parks, trails, neighborhoods, the lakefront, outdoor events and plenty of places to explore. Use the same backpack for biking, hiking, outdoor studying, weekend plans, day trips or meeting friends around the city.
