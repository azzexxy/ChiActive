Why Chicago College Students Need a Faux-Fur Aviator Hat

Written by Mariia Dovhoshyya in Accessories · September 20, 2026

Chicago winters are cold in a way that surprises people who move here for school. It's not just the temperature — it's the wind that comes off the lake and finds every inch of exposed skin. For students who spend their days walking to class, waiting for the bus, or standing on an open L platform, the ChiActive Faux-Fur Aviator Hat, at $51.99, is the piece of gear that makes the cold bearable.

The wind is the real enemy

Anyone can layer up for a cold day. What breaks you in Chicago is wind chill, and it hits your head and ears first. A hood helps, but it slips down, blocks your vision when you check the street, and gets pushed around by any gust. An aviator hat is designed specifically for this: a snug fit that stays put, flaps that actually cover your ears, and a faux-fur trim that cuts the wind at the edges rather than letting it whistle straight past. When you're standing still waiting for the 151, that difference is everything.

That's the whole philosophy the brand runs on: the lake wind doesn't care about your plans. Your gear should.

Warm ears change your whole commute

Cold ears don't just feel bad — they make the entire trip worse. Once your head is warm, you stop counting the minutes until the next train and start actually noticing the walk. That ten-minute trek from your dorm to the first lecture of the day becomes normal instead of miserable. For commuter students riding the CTA across the city, a hat like this is the difference between arriving at class alert and arriving frozen. It's the smallest piece of winter gear you own, and it might be the one you'd miss the most if you forgot it.

Why the aviator design works on campus

  • Ear flaps that stay down. Keep your ears covered on the worst days without holding them in place.
  • Faux-fur trim. Blocks wind at the edges and keeps warmth from escaping around your face.
  • One size, easy fit. Pull it on over any hairstyle and you're out the door in seconds.
  • Looks great with everything. It pairs naturally with the Always Effortless Jacket and the Chi Town Puffer.

Built for the four-season city

Chicago gives you two months of genuine cold and several more of unpredictable chill. This hat is built for the four-season city, ready for the deep freeze of January and the raw, windy mornings of early spring alike. Wear it on its own when it's brisk, or tuck it into your bag as backup for the days that turn colder than the forecast promised.

A small accessory with an outsized payoff

It's easy to spend a semester pairing a great jacket with a cheap beanie that leaves your ears stinging. The Aviator Hat fixes that gap. At $51.99, it's a one-time purchase that upgrades every cold walk you take until spring. Warm ears, a clear head, and a look that works from the classroom to the coffee shop — that's a lot of value for a small piece of gear.

Don't let a Chicago winter keep you indoors or complaining. Dress for it properly, and the cold becomes just another part of your daily routine.
