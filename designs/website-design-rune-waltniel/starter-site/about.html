About ChiActive

Chicago isn’t a one-season city, and your gear shouldn’t be either. ChiActive makes authentic athletic apparel and outdoor equipment for college students who move between the lecture hall, the L platform, the lakefront trail, and the weekend trailhead — often in the same week.

Our Story

ChiActive started with a simple observation: students in Chicago spend a huge amount of their day outside, and most of the gear available to them is either built for a gym or built for a mountain — rarely for the specific reality of a Tuesday in February.

Between early classes, walking across campus, waiting for the bus, and exploring the city after school, there is a lot of weather to survive. So we build for that: water-resistant cargos, wind-blocking shells, insulated boots, and packs that can take a surprise rain shower on the walk home.

Who We Make Gear For

Our products are designed for college students balancing classes, city commuting, and four-season outdoor adventures. That means practical storage, honest materials, fits that work over layers, and prices that make sense on a student budget.

Whether you are biking to class, riding the CTA, studying near the lakefront, or driving out to a state park for the weekend, the goal is the same: gear you don’t have to think about.

FAQ

How long does shipping take?

Standard shipping arrives in 3–5 business days. Orders over $75 ship free. This site is a student design project, so no real orders are placed.

What is your return policy?

Unworn items can be returned within 30 days, no questions asked. Bring them to any campus pop-up or ship them back.

Do you offer a student discount?

Yes — verify your student status at checkout and the discount applies automatically.

How should I care for water-resistant gear?

Machine wash cold on a gentle cycle and hang dry. Skip the fabric softener, which can break down the water-repellent finish.
