The Urban Campus Backpack

The ChiActive Urban Campus Backpack is designed for college students who need a reliable backpack for classes, commuting, biking, studying outdoors and exploring Chicago.

Chicago weather can change quickly. From light rain and wind to busy train rides and long walks across campus, students need a backpack that keeps up with their routine.

Key features

Weather-resistant exterior

Water-resistant recycled polyester helps protect books, electronics and everyday essentials from light rain.

Padded laptop compartment

A dedicated padded compartment holds laptops up to 15.6 inches.

25-liter capacity

Room for textbooks, notebooks, chargers, headphones, gym clothes and snacks.

Comfortable straps and bottle pockets

Padded, adjustable shoulder straps and two side pockets for water bottles.

Lightweight design

At only 1.8 pounds, it adds storage without adding weight.
