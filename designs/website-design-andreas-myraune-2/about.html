Made for the walk to class.

ChiActive designs jackets for college students in Chicago: people who walk to class in January, wait on the L with the wind coming off the lake, and still want to be outside on the weekend.

Collection 02 is six quiet jackets named after Chicago streets and neighborhoods: the Clark Shell, Lakeview Parka, Halsted Down Jacket, Kinzie Overshirt, Racine Fleece and the Ohio Street Rain Coat.

Every jacket is cut clean, in calm colours, and carries one thing on the front: the ChiActive patch, with the red six-pointed star of the Chicago flag over the mountains we escape to.

About this site

A practice storefront for the Intro to Interactive Marketing course. The product and campaign photos were generated with AI (Canva), and the real ChiActive patch was printed onto the jackets afterwards. The cart and checkout are demos: nothing ships and no payment is taken.

ChiActive, Chicago. Built for the four-season city.
