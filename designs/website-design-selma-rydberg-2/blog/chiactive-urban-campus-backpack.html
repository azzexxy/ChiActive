ChiActive Urban Campus Backpack

Written by Vaidehi Chavda in Uncategorized · September 23, 2026

Built for Campus. Ready for Chicago. — $59.99

The ChiActive Urban Campus Backpack is designed for college students who need a reliable backpack for classes, commuting, biking, studying outdoors, and exploring Chicago.

Chicago weather can change quickly. From light rain and wind to busy train rides and long walks across campus, students need a backpack that can keep up with their everyday routine. The ChiActive Urban Campus Backpack combines durability, comfort, storage, and weather protection in one modern design.

Key features

  • Weather-resistant exterior — water-resistant recycled polyester protects books, electronics, and everyday essentials from light rain.
  • Padded laptop compartment — safely holds laptops up to 15.6 inches.
  • 25-litre capacity — room for textbooks, notebooks, chargers, headphones, gym clothes, and snacks.
  • Comfortable adjustable straps — padded for long walks around campus or commuting.
  • Dual water-bottle pockets — quick access to bottles and drink containers.
  • Lightweight design — just 1.8 pounds of useful storage.

Made for Chicago college life

Whether you're walking through campus, riding the CTA, biking to class, or spending the afternoon studying near the lakefront, the ChiActive Urban Campus Backpack is designed to move with you. Its simple black design works with almost any style while providing the practical features students need every day.

Chicago weather can change quickly

Chicago students experience everything from sunny afternoons to rain, wind, snow, and changing temperatures throughout the school year. When you're carrying a laptop, textbooks, and chargers, unexpected weather can become a problem — a weather-resistant backpack adds a layer of protection against light rain and moisture.

Designed for commuting

Chicago has thousands of students who commute by train, bus, bicycle, or a combination. With roughly 25 litres of storage and a 1.8-pound design, the ChiActive backpack carries daily essentials without being bulky.

Useful beyond the classroom

Chicago offers parks, trails, neighbourhoods, lakefront areas, and plenty of places to explore. Use the same backpack for biking, hiking, outdoor studying, weekend activities, or meeting friends around the city.
