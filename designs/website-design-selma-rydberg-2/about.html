Our story

ChiActive started with a simple, familiar aggravation: leaving a dorm or apartment at 8 a.m. in a jacket that surrendered to the first gust off the lake. Four seasons in Chicago aren't decorative — they're a design brief. So we built gear around the commute, the lecture hall, the lakefront path, and the weekend trail, then priced it for people paying tuition.

The patch on every piece says EST. CHICAGO, and it means it: the star, the skyline, the sky blue, the four-season city.

Who we make gear for

Students who walk twenty minutes to the L in January. Students who bike to class and study outside on the first warm day of April. Students who go from a 3 p.m. seminar to an 8 p.m. trailhead with one bag.

  • Four-season by default. Water-resistant shells, wind-resistant layers, breathable fabrics.
  • Campus-first design. Pockets that hold a phone, a CTA card, and a charger — plus a padded laptop sleeve where it counts.
  • Student pricing. 15% off with a student email, plus free pickup on campus.
