ChiActive Urban Campus Backpack

Written by Vaidehi Chavda in Uncategorized · September 23, 2026

Built for Campus. Ready for Chicago.

Price: $59.99

The ChiActive Urban Campus Backpack is designed for college students who need a reliable backpack for classes, commuting, biking, studying outdoors, and exploring Chicago.

Chicago weather can change quickly. From light rain and wind to busy train rides and long walks across campus, students need a backpack that can keep up with their everyday routine. The ChiActive Urban Campus Backpack combines durability, comfort, storage, and weather protection in one modern design.

Key Features

Weather-Resistant Exterior

Made from water-resistant recycled polyester to help protect books, electronics, and everyday essentials from light rain and changing weather.

Padded Laptop Compartment

A dedicated padded compartment safely holds laptops up to 15.6 inches, making it ideal for students carrying computers between classes.

25-Liter Capacity

The spacious main compartment provides room for textbooks, notebooks, chargers, headphones, gym clothes, snacks, and other college essentials.

Comfortable Adjustable Straps

Padded, adjustable shoulder straps provide additional comfort during long walks around campus or while commuting.

Dual Water-Bottle Pockets

Convenient side pockets make it easy to carry water bottles, reusable drink containers, or other quick-access items.

Lightweight Design

At only 1.8 pounds, the backpack provides useful storage without adding unnecessary weight.

Made for Chicago College Life

Whether you're walking through campus, riding the CTA, biking to class, or spending the afternoon studying near the lakefront, the ChiActive Urban Campus Backpack is designed to move with you.

Chicago Weather Can Change Quickly

Chicago students experience everything from sunny afternoons to rain, wind, snow, and changing temperatures throughout the school year. When you're carrying a laptop, textbooks, notebooks, headphones, chargers, and other important items, unexpected weather can become a problem. Designed for Commuting

Chicago has thousands of students who commute to school using trains, buses, bicycles, or a combination of transportation methods. Chicago offers parks, trails, neighborhoods, lakefront areas, outdoor events, and plenty of places for students to explore. Students can use the same backpack for biking, hiking, outdoor studying, weekend activities, day trips, or simply meeting friends around the city.
