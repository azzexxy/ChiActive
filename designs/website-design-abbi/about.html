About ChiActive

Built for the four-season city. This gear keeps up when the day starts in the classroom and ends at the lakeshore. Designed for you and whatever Chicago adventures you choose.

Our Story

ChiActive was founded by a class of college students (just like you!) who were dressing for two lives at once. One outfit for class, on the train, and on the windy lakefront. We decided to make practical gear that makes life in Chicago easy and fun.

Who We Make Gear For

You! A Chicago college student who balances classes, commuting, and the city life. Your gear should be ready, even when the forecast is not.
