About ChiActive

Built for the four-season city.

Our Story

ChiActive was born out of a real problem every Chicago student knows too well: you step out of your morning class in mild sunshine, and by the time you're catching the train home, the temperature has plunged 25 degrees and the wind off Lake Michigan feels like liquid ice.

Most outdoor gear on the market felt either too technical and clunky for university life, or cheap streetwear that soaked through in the first November rain. We set out to bridge the gap: apparel engineered with technical mountain-grade weatherproofing, shaped for student mobility, comfort, and the unmistakable energy of Chicago.

“The lake wind doesn't care about your plans. Your jacket should.”

Who We Make Gear For

ChiActive is designed for college students who want clothing and gear that can keep up with fast-paced city life and outdoor activities. Since students are often balancing class schedules, cross-town commutes, library study sessions, and weekend adventures outside the city, ChiActive focuses on a style that feels practical, youthful, and authentically connected to Chicago.

Our Core Design Pillars

Four-Season Versatility

Lightweight layering systems that transition effortlessly from 60° autumn days to sub-zero polar plunges.

Student Durability

Reinforced seams, water-repellent coatings, and padded laptop compartments that withstand daily abuse.
