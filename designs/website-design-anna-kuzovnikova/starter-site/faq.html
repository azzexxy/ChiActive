🌬️ Weatherproofing & Chicago Winter Survival

Are ChiActive jackets actually warm enough for sub-zero wind chills? ›
Yes! Our outerwear is specifically tested along the Lake Michigan shoreline in mid-January. Models like the Green Winter Expedition Jacket and Chi Town Puffer feature high-density thermal insulation and wind-blocking baffle stitching calibrated to stop 35+ MPH lakefront gusts dead in their tracks.
What is DWR water repellency on the Trail Cargo Pants? ›
DWR stands for Durable Water Repellent. It’s a microscopic coating applied to our stretch nylon ripstop fabric that causes sudden Chicago sleet, freezing rain, and melted slush to bead up and roll right off without soaking through your pants on your walk to class.
Will my laptop stay safe inside the Urban Campus Backpack during rain? ›
Yes. The backpack is engineered from water-resistant recycled polyester and features an elevated, padded laptop cradle that keeps laptops up to 15.6" suspended above the bottom of the bag, protecting your tech from sudden storms and wet bus seats alike.

📏 Sizing, Layering & Student Returns

How do ChiActive clothes fit? Should I size up for heavy layers? ›
All ChiActive apparel is designed with an athletic streetwear silhouette that fits true to size while intentionally leaving subtle room for base thermal layers or hoodies underneath. If you prefer an oversized skate/streetwear fit, feel free to size up one tier!
What is your return and exchange policy? ›
We offer a stress-free 30-day student return window. Items must be unwashed with tags intact. Plus, we offer free size exchanges during midterms and finals week so you don't have to stress about ill-fitting gear while studying.
How should I wash and care for water-resistant gear? ›
Machine wash cold on gentle cycle with mild detergent. Avoid fabric softeners (which break down the DWR coating), and tumble dry on low heat for 15 minutes to reactivate the water-repellent finish. Easy to do in any dorm laundry room!
