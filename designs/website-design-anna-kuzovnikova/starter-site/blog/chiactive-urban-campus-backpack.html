The ChiActive Urban Campus Backpack is designed for college students who need a reliable backpack for classes, commuting, biking, studying outdoors, and exploring Chicago.

Chicago weather can change quickly. From sudden rains along Michigan Avenue to cramped CTA train rides and sprints between campus quad buildings, students need gear that won't let them down. The ChiActive Urban Campus Backpack combines extreme durability, ergonomic comfort, organized compartments, and weather protection into one sleek package.

Key Technical Features

Weather-Resistant Exterior

Constructed from water-resistant recycled polyester to protect laptops, electronics, and notebooks from wet weather.

Padded Laptop Sleeve

Dedicated suspended compartment fits laptops up to 15.6" with shock-absorbing foam lining.

25-Liter Storage Capacity

Generous main compartment easily swallows textbooks, gym layers, chargers, and lunch boxes.

Dual Bottle Pockets

Elastic reinforced mesh pockets secure large thermal water bottles and folding umbrellas.
