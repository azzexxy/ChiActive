# Brand Guidelines
## Who is ChiActive
- ChiActive is designed for college students who want clothing and gear that can keep up with city life and outdoor activities. Since students are often balancing classes, commuting, and having adventures outside of class, ChiActive focuses on a style that feels practical, youthful, and connected to Chicago.  

## Colors

- Secondary Color: #2F3F53

### Accent Colors

## Brand Voice
- Direct, encouraging, outdoorsy, energetic.
