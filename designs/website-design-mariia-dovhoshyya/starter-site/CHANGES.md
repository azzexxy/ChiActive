# ChiActive design update

## Design
A minimal, modern retail layout inspired by the editorial approach of Canada Goose, using ChiActive's own logo, brand fonts, product images, and copy.

- Full-width outdoor photography leads the homepage, with concise text and two clear shopping links.
- White backgrounds, generous spacing, smaller navigation, understated product cards, square buttons, and fine dividers simplify the visual hierarchy.
- Finlandica headings and Google Sans body text load locally. The approved brand palette remains in use: navy buttons, a subtle sky-blue header line and focus states, red sale labels, and cream hover accents.
- A compact category menu connects all six categories: Jackets, Pants, Shoes, Accessories, Gear, and Layers.
- Consistent headers and footers appear on all 17 pages. Responsive layouts adapt the hero, navigation, product grids, and product details to smaller screens.
- Supplied product prices and images remain intact. Category pages and extra product previews connect the original placeholder categories. New previews use only available product information.
- The homepage journal feature links directly to the supplied Urban Campus Backpack article.
