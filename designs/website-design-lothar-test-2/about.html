Started on a cold bus stop

North Branch began with one question: why is warm gear so expensive when you need it most as a student?

Our story

In our first Chicago winter we learned that the lake wind makes 25°F feel like 10°F, and that most “student” jackets gave up by December.

So we started testing gear on real commutes: the Red Line platform at 7 am, the bike lane on Milwaukee Avenue, the walk across the quad in sleet. The pieces that survived became the North Branch collection.

Today we sell twelve products, run free group hikes every month and answer every message ourselves.

What we care about

Honest prices

We list what a product costs and why. No fake “was” prices.

Gear that lasts

Every seam is tested for a full winter before a product goes on sale.

Less waste

Recycled fabrics where we can, and repairs before replacements.

Open to everyone

Hikes are free and paced for beginners. Nobody walks alone at the back.

How we got here

  1. 2024First prototype jacket sewn in a dorm room and tested on the Lakefront Trail.
  2. 2025First pop-up at the student union sells out of beanies in two hours.
  3. 2026Twelve products, monthly group hikes and a website designed for the ChiActive class project.
