Questions we hear a lot

Still wondering about something? Send us a message and a real student will answer.

Do you offer a student discount?

Yes. Use code NORTH10 for 10% off your first order, and join the newsletter for member prices during finals week.

How does shipping work?

Orders over $60 ship free. Under $60, shipping is a flat $5.95. Most orders arrive within three to five business days.

Can I return something?

Unworn items can be returned within 30 days. Print the label from your confirmation email and drop it at any post office.

Who can join the group hikes?

Any student, any fitness level. Every hike has a leader at the front and one at the back, so nobody gets left behind.

What should I wear in January?

Three layers: a base layer, a fleece and a windproof shell or puffer. Add a beanie and gloves, because the lake wind is the real temperature.
