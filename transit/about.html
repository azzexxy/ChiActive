Our story

We're a Chicago brand built for students who commute, study, explore and do it all in weather that changes by the hour.

Why we exist

Good gear shouldn’t cost a semester of textbooks.

ChiActive started with a simple problem: Chicago students walk, bike, wait for trains and head out on weekend adventures in some of the toughest weather in the country, but most outdoor brands are priced for people with full-time salaries.

So we make practical, good-looking clothing and gear that handles lake wind, slush and surprise downpours, at prices that make sense on a student budget. Everything is chosen for city life first and the trail second, because most days you need both.
